Walking around town

While you are in town, your position and display name are sent live to people nearby. This isn't stored.

Who else sees it

Other visitors see your username on your house unless you hide it. Our providers: Vercel (hosting) and Supabase (database and live updates). Instagram (Meta) handles the messages you send us there. We don't sell your data.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: My house → ⚙️ → Delete my house and account. It happens immediately.
  • Can't sign in? Message us and we will delete your house.
